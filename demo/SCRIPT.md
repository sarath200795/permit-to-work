## Full narration (plain text)

Welcome to Permit to Work. Control high-risk work — dual-team approvals, auto-populated hazards, and an 8-hour permit lifecycle.

In this short tutorial, we'll walk through how to use the app.

First, register your organization. The first person to sign up becomes the administrator.

Enter your details and a password, then create the organization.

Permit dashboard. Permits by status, recent activity and work-type breakdown.

Raise a permit. Pick a work type — hazards, PPE, precautions and required documents auto-populate.

All permits. Filter by status, type and site; see approvers and the expiry countdown.

Approvals. Engineering and Operations approve independently before work begins.

Observations. Safety observations and suggestions raised against permits.

Permit to Work — no high-risk job without sign-off.. Start by registering your organization.

That's a quick tour of Permit to Work. Thanks for watching — get started by registering your organization.
